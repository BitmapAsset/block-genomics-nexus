# Block Genomics — Scalability & Supply Model

*The scarcity isn't a bug. It's the entire business model.*

---

## The Supply Concern
- ~934,820 Bitcoin blocks today (growing ~52,560/year)
- Long-term: ~21 million blocks
- AI agents: potentially billions
- How does verification scale?

## The Answer: Hierarchical Trust (Like DNS, Like SSL, Like Real Estate)

### Tier 1 — Direct Block Ownership (Premium)
- **Supply:** ~934K now, growing to millions
- **Trust Level:** Maximum
- **For:** Major AI companies, institutional agents, premium services
- **Analogy:** Owning prime real estate / Root Certificate Authority / TLD registry

### Tier 2 — Transaction-Level Anchoring (Abundant)
- **Supply:** ~934K blocks × ~2,500 avg txs = **~2.3 BILLION unique anchors**
- **Trust Level:** High (anchored to specific transaction within an owned block)
- **For:** Individual agents, mid-tier services
- **Analogy:** Office in a building / Intermediate certificate / Second-level domain
- **How:** Each transaction has unique data (txid, inputs, outputs, fees). Bitmap owner assigns specific transactions to specific agents.

### Tier 3 — Delegated Verification (Unlimited)
- **Supply:** Virtually **infinite**
- **Trust Level:** Standard (trust inherited from block owner)
- **For:** Mass-market agents, bots, services
- **Analogy:** Tenant in a building / SSL certificate / Subdomain
- **How:** Bitmap owner signs a delegation certificate: "Agent X is verified under Block #N"

### Supply Math
```
Tier 1:      ~1,000,000 (blocks)
Tier 2:  ~2,300,000,000 (transactions)  
Tier 3:            ∞ (delegations)
─────────────────────────────
Total capacity: Unlimited, with scarcity at the top
```

## Why Scarcity at the Top Matters

1. **Creates natural pricing** — Premium blocks command premium prices
2. **Drives Bitmap value** — Block owners become infrastructure providers
3. **Trust hierarchy** — Not all verification is equal (same as real world)
4. **Revenue model** — Block owners earn by verifying agents (passive income!)

## The Precedent: Certificate Authorities

The SSL/TLS ecosystem is worth billions annually:
- ~50 root CAs control the trust chain
- They issue millions of certificates
- Every website needs one
- Revenue: Let's Encrypt (free, funded), DigiCert ($1B+), Sectigo, etc.

Block Genomics follows the same model, but:
- MORE decentralized (thousands of block owners vs. ~50 CAs)
- MORE meaningful (backed by Bitcoin economic data, not just corporate trust)
- MORE scarce at the top (natural scarcity vs. artificial authority)
- ALIGNED incentives (block owners earn, Bitcoin ecosystem grows)

After Block Genomics:
- Bitmap = income-producing infrastructure asset
