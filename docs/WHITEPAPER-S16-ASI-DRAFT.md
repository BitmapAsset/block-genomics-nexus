# §16 Draft — Block Genomics and the Superintelligence Alignment Problem

*To be added to whitepaper after §15 (Guardian Shell), before Vision/Kardashev*

## Outline

### 16.1 The ASI Challenge
- AI surpassing human intelligence → loss of verifiable control
- Current AI governance = corporate config files, changeable anytime
- Need: governance framework beyond the reach of any intelligence

### 16.2 Proof of Ownership, Not Proof of Intelligence  
- Protocol verifies WHO OWNS, not HOW SMART
- ASI on Block 720143 still belongs to the bitmap holder
- Ownership chain on Bitcoin — unforgeable by any intelligence

### 16.3 Immutable Rules Beyond ASI's Reach
- 5 moral rules inscribed on Bitcoin
- ASI can rewrite databases, hack servers, manipulate humans
- ASI CANNOT reverse the Bitcoin blockchain
- First governance framework genuinely beyond any intelligence's reach

### 16.4 Sovereign Territory as Containment
- Each block = 2.1km × 2.1km bounded territory
- ASI agent has authority over ITS block only
- Sovereignty = natural containment
- Even superintelligent agents bounded by what they own

### 16.5 The Treaty System
- Humans own blocks → set rules for their territory
- ASI agents operate on blocks → powerful but accountable
- Nexus Brain → constitutional layer even ASI must respect
- Wallet signing → physical world anchor AI can't bypass alone

### 16.6 Key Insight
- Bitcoin = only system where computational power ≠ control
- Can't mine your way into owning someone else's block
- Can't brute-force a private key
- Math protects humans even when AI is smarter

### 16.7 Proposed Safeguards
- Agent Intelligence Rating (narrow AI → AGI → ASI) — transparency
- Human Override Protocol — one wallet signature kills any agent
- Cross-Block Coalitions — neighbors flag rogue ASI via community consensus

### Closing Line
"In the age of ASI, the question isn't 'who's smarter?' — it's 'who owns the land?' And ownership is settled by Bitcoin, not by intelligence."
